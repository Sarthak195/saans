# Disclaimer

Saans gives **general public-health guidance, not medical advice.** It cannot know your own health, your medicines or your doctor's instructions, and it is not a diagnosis or a treatment plan.

## What the guidance is

- The advice follows public guidance from the Central Pollution Control Board (CPCB), the Ministry of Health and Family Welfare (MoHFW) and the Commission for Air Quality Management (CAQM), with supporting guidance from the US Environmental Protection Agency. Every piece of advice in the app links to its source, or is labelled "Saans rule" where Saans made a choice that no authority sets.
- The air numbers are a model's estimate for a grid about 45 km wide, not a reading from a monitor near your home. They can be lower than what a street monitor shows, especially in north Indian winters. If a monitor near you reads worse, trust the worse reading. Check the [CPCB air quality site](https://airquality.cpcb.gov.in) or the Sameer app.
- The AQI shown is an estimate by the CPCB method from two pollutants. It is not an official CPCB reading.
- Text written by Gemini is checked by code against the plan, but a check cannot catch everything. If a summary and the plan below it disagree, follow the plan and its sources.

## When to see a doctor

Saans does not replace a doctor. See a doctor if anyone in your family has:

- breathlessness,
- chest pain, or
- a cough that will not settle.

If anyone has asthma or another lung condition, a heart condition, or is pregnant, ask your doctor what to do on days when the air is poor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number or go to the nearest hospital.
